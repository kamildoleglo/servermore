Questions

Can I keep working while my machine is in production?

No. We recommend getting a second machine. Once your code works on that one, we'll ship it too.

How do I ship a hotfix?

Write the fix on your new machine, then we ship that machine and the customer returns the old one. Zero-downtime deploys require the customer to own two desks.

Does it scale to a million users?

Yes, with a million laptops. Volume discounts start at 500 units, and our warehouse partners have been briefed.

Is it GDPR compliant?

Your data never leaves your machine. Your machine leaves you. Our lawyers are still deciding which of those matters.

What about Kubernetes?

We ship the cluster as well. It's three Raspberry Pis held together with packing tape. They work on our machine.

What happened to Dave's laptop?

Dave closed the lid to go to lunch. Production slept with it. We've added a sticky note that says "don't" and we consider the incident resolved.

Is this real?

About as real as your staging environment. Please don't actually post us your laptop.
